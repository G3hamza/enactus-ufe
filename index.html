Know Enactus

What is Enactus?

Enactus is a global network of students in 30+ countries who use business to solve real social and environmental problems. At UFE, we build our own projects, test them with real people, and compete at the Enactus Egypt National Competition.

Ni une association caritative, ni des cours : une équipe d'étudiants qui construit de vrais projets pour changer de vraies vies.

What you get

  • Skills your courses don't teach: leadership, project management, pitching, teamwork.
  • Real work for your CV: projects, events and partnerships you actually ran.
  • A national stage: pitch in front of business leaders at the national competition.
  • Weekly sessions that get you job-ready, plus a network of alumni.
  • Your people: a team you'll keep long after university.

Quick answers

Do I need experience?

No. Everyone gets onboarding and training in their team. We care about commitment, not CVs.

How much time does it take?

A few hours a week plus one weekly meeting. We plan around your exams, study comes first.

I'm in first year. Too early?

It's the best time. First-years grow the most, and today's members are next year's Heads.

What happens after I sign up?

We message you on WhatsApp within 48 hours with the next step: a short, friendly chat and the welcome session date.

Is there a fee?

No. Joining Enactus UFE is free.
